About the Data Science with AI & ML Certificate

A comprehensive, rigorous postgraduate online programme designed and delivered by the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Asian Technology Hub (ATH). The curriculum is meticulously structured to take engineering and data professionals from fundamental mathematical intuition to advanced, production-scale AI applications.

Unlike superficial short courses, ATH’s Executive Certificate focuses on First Principles engineering: understanding the underlying gradient dynamics of transformers, fine-tuning large models with parameter-efficient techniques (LoRA/QLoRA), designing resilient Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines, and deploying autonomous multi-agent swarms with enterprise observability.

100% Live Instructor-Led Classes: Interactive weekend sessions with top machine learning scientists and system architects.
Production-First Mindset: Every algorithm is implemented with real-world latency, throughput, token budgeting, and cloud cost constraints.

In-Depth Syllabus

The Full-Stack Data Science with AI & ML Curriculum

A 3–4 month intensive journey structured to turn you into a production-grade Data Scientist and AI Systems Engineer.

Pre-Cohort Bridge (Weeks 1 - 3)

AI Foundations & Mathematical Bridge

24 Live Hours

Build an rock-solid intuition in computational mathematics and programming essentials. Designed to align learners coming from diverse technical backgrounds, software engineering, and analytical disciplines.

Key Concepts & Hands-on Labs
Advanced Vectorized Python (NumPy, Pandas, Vector Ops)
Linear Algebra: Matrices, Eigenvalues, SVD, Dot Products
Multivariable Calculus & Gradient Descent Intuition
Probability Distributions, Bayes' Rule & MLE / MAP
Descriptive & Inferential Statistics for Data Science
SQL for AI: Complex joins, Window functions & Aggregations
Hands-on Milestone: High-Throughput Matrix Engine

Implement an autograd engine from scratch in pure Python and NumPy to comprehend backward propagation.

Module 1 (Weeks 4 - 8)

Machine Learning Foundations & Production Pipelines

32 Live Hours

Dive deep into algorithmic decision boundaries, loss functions, regularization, and feature engineering. Learn how models generalize and deploy production pipelines.

Key Concepts & Hands-on Labs
Supervised Learning: Regression, Logistic, SVMs, Decision Trees
Ensemble Methods: Random Forests, XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost
Unsupervised Learning: K-Means, DBSCAN, t-SNE, PCA
Feature Engineering & Automated Data Preprocessing Pipelines
Model Evaluation: ROC-AUC, PR Curves, Precision/Recall trade-offs
Hyperparameter Optimization with Optuna & Cross-Validation
Deployed System 1: Real-Time Fraud & Anomaly Prediction API

Build and package an end-to-end streaming ML pipeline predicting high-risk transactions with sub-50ms latency.

Module 2 (Weeks 9 - 13)

Deep Learning Architectures & Computer Vision

36 Live Hours

Master PyTorch fundamentals, backpropagation dynamics, convolutional neural networks (CNNs), transfer learning, and modern computer vision systems.

Key Concepts & Hands-on Labs
PyTorch Deep Dive: Tensors, Autograd, Custom Datasets & Dataloaders
Multi-Layer Perceptrons, Activation Functions & Vanishing Gradients
CNN Architectures: ResNet, EfficientNet, ConvNeXt
Real-Time Object Detection with YOLOv8 & Segment Anything (SAM)
Transfer Learning, Fine-Tuning & Learning Rate Schedulers
Optimization: AdamW, Batch Normalization, Dropout & Augmentations
Deployed System 2: Autonomous Quality Inspection & Vision Pipeline

Train and deploy a high-speed defect segmentation model with TorchScript & ONNX runtime on low-latency cloud endpoints.

Module 3 (Weeks 14 - 17)

NLP, Transformers & Speech Intelligence

30 Live Hours

Understand how machines comprehend sequence data and natural text. From tokenization and self-attention mechanisms to modern Transformer architectures and Whisper speech models.

Key Concepts & Hands-on Labs
Word Embeddings (Word2Vec, FastText) & Tokenization (BPE, WordPiece)
The Transformer Blueprint: Self-Attention & Multi-Head Attention
BERT, RoBERTa, DeBERTa for Classification & Entity Extraction
Hugging Face Transformers, Datasets & Tokenizers Libraries
Speech AI: OpenAI Whisper for Multilingual Audio Transcription
Cross-Lingual & Low-Resource Language Processing (Indic NLP)
Deployed System 3: Multilingual Voice & Intent Analytics Engine

Speech-to-text pipeline that parses multi-speaker customer conversations, detects intent, and generates structured summarization.

Module 4 (Weeks 18 - 21)

Generative AI, Prompt Engineering & LLM Adaptation

32 Live Hours

Transition from consuming public APIs to mastering local open-source weights (Llama 3, Mistral, Gemma). Learn prompt engineering architectures, structured JSON outputs, and parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT/LoRA).

Key Concepts & Hands-on Labs
Advanced Prompt Engineering (CoT, ReAct, Few-Shot, Tree of Thoughts)
Structured Generation with Pydantic, Instructor & Outlines
Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning: LoRA, QLoRA, Prefix Tuning
Quantization Methods: GGUF, AWQ, GPTQ for Edge & Local Inference
Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) & Reinforcement Learning from AI Feedback
LLM Evaluation Benchmarks (MT-Bench, ROUGE, BLEU, G-Eval)
Deployed System 4: Custom Domain-Adapted Specialist LLM

Fine-tune an open-source 8B LLM using QLoRA on proprietary legal/medical documents and deploy with vLLM.

Module 5 (Weeks 22 - 25)

Enterprise RAG Systems & Vector Search

30 Live Hours

Solve LLM hallucinations for business applications. Build resilient, enterprise-grade Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems with hybrid vector search, rerankers, and guardrails.

Key Concepts & Hands-on Labs
Vector Databases in Production: Milvus, Pinecone, Qdrant, Chroma
Chunking Strategies: Semantic, Hierarchical, Parent-Document & Sentence Window
Hybrid Search: BM25 Keyword Search + Dense Vector Embeddings
Cross-Encoder Rerankers (Cohere Rerank, BGE-Reranker)
RAG Evaluation with RAGAS Framework (Faithfulness, Context Relevance)
Safety & Guardrails: NeMo Guardrails, Llama-Guard & PII Masking
Deployed System 5: Production Enterprise RAG Knowledge Engine

Multi-tenant document analysis engine handling complex PDFs, tables, and financial reports with citations and verified guardrails.

Module 6 (Weeks 26 - 29)

Agentic AI, Multi-Agent Swarms & MLOps

34 Live Hours

Step into the frontier of AI: autonomous agents capable of planning, invoking external tools, writing and executing code, and collaborating in multi-agent frameworks. Complete the loop with CI/CD and MLOps deployment.

Key Concepts & Hands-on Labs
Agent Architectures: Planning, Memory Systems & Tool Execution
LangGraph & CrewAI: Multi-Agent Collaboration & State Management
Model Context Protocol (MCP) & Function Calling Standards
Dockerizing AI Services & FastAPI Async High-Concurrency Servers
MLOps: MLflow for Experiment Tracking, Prometheus & Grafana Monitoring
High-Throughput Inference with vLLM, TensorRT-LLM & Ray Serve
Deployed System 6: Autonomous Research & Code Execution Agent Swarm

Multi-agent team that crawls web data, queries databases, synthesizes reports, and writes verified Python code autonomously.

Final Milestone (Weeks 30 - 32)

Production Capstone: Build & Launch in Your Domain

40 Mentored Hours

Bring everything together in a comprehensive, real-world industry capstone. Work under the direct guidance of an assigned AI Architect mentor, solving an unsolved domain problem in Healthcare, Banking/FinTech, Supply Chain, or Retail.

Capstone Deliverables & Standards
Formal Technical Architecture & RFC Design Document
End-to-End Pipeline: Data Ingestion to Model Inference
Production Cloud Deployment with Public Domain & SSL
Comprehensive Latency, Cost & Hallucination Benchmark Report
